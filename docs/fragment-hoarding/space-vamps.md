### Material Goods Unique to a Space-Vampire Economy  

| Item | Vampire‑Specific Aspect | Market Value Drivers |
|------|--------------------------|----------------------|
| **Radiation Crystals** (harvested from supernova cores, pulsars) | Primary sustenance fuel for vampires; glows faint violet when unshielded. | Supply cap: nova explosions occur ~once every decade → scarcity spikes every ten-year cycle → black-market multiplies price 3–10× compared to civilian grid. |
| **Soul Nectar Extracts** (condensed emotional energy harvested from human settlers during periods of fear) | Appears as amber liquid stored in crystal vials; humans develop “hollowed-out” memories if injected. | Demand is elite vampires; competition among noble houses drives **authenticity certification**; forged extracts cause revulsion attacks instead of sustenance. |
| **Quantum Cloaking Circuits** (hardware implants) | Tailored for undead biologies → consumes residual starlight to render user electromagnetic-invisible. | License restricted to vampire nobility → gray-market kits cost up to 500,000 credits per circuit; smuggling attempts trigger orbital patrols. |
| **Chrono-Stasis Cabinets** | Preserves vampiric tissue indefinitely (slows decay caused by starlight exposure). | Luxury item; warships keep one on board for medical emergencies; black-market resale valued at equivalent weight of platinum plus diplomatic leverage. |

### Revenue Models & Currency Ideas  

1. **Shadow Credits (ShCr)** – untraceable token, embedded with quantum hush chips.  
   • Mining pools shared by vampire syndicate servers across deep space; drops are timestamped but **cannot be linked to real IPs**.
   • Denominated in decimal increments down to 0.00001 ShCr; minted each cycle by harvesting photon pulses from novas.

2. **Sun Tithe** – mandatory “star-duty” paid in raw radiation crystals by colonies under vampire protectorates → balances are kept in shielded vaults orbiting dying white dwarfs.

3. **Life Debt Ledgers** – blockchain-like registry tracking borrowed life-force exchanges. Humans sell time-extensions for credits, debts auto‑accrue interest if not repaid in kind within 72 rotations.

| **Black Wharf Belt** | Interstellar “neutral” asteroid belt market where any race trades | Neutral customs but vampiric spies = **escrow fraud** potential; ships often insure cargo against soul-poaching |
| **Stellar Sanctuary Corridors** (protected by vampire armada) | Safe lanes for regulated soul nectar flow | Low tariffs, controlled quantity → stable base market for middle-tier vampires |
